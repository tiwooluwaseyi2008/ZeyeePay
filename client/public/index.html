Fair questions

Before you fund your wallet

How fast does airtime or data actually arrive?

Most transactions complete in under 3 seconds. If a network is genuinely down on the provider's end, we hold your money and refund it automatically — you're never left guessing.

What happens if a transaction fails?

Failed transactions are reversed to your wallet automatically, usually within a minute. You can also track every transaction's status live from your dashboard.

Can I resell airtime and data to my own customers?

Yes. Reseller accounts unlock lower per-unit rates and an API you can plug into your own site or bot.

Is my wallet money safe?

Funds are held with a licensed payment processor and every transaction is logged and reversible. Support responds within minutes, not days.
